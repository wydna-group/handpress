# Sample copy-texts

## `ado/`

Old-spelling transcriptions of *Much Ado About Nothing*, used to calibrate the
simulation. Five scenes (2.1, 2.3, 3.1, 4.1, 5.1) in two states:

- `q1600-*.txt` — the 1600 quarto, printed by Valentine Simmes for Andrew Wise
  and William Aspley
- `f1623-*.txt` — the 1623 First Folio text, set from an annotated exemplar of
  that quarto
- `_all-q1600.txt` — the five quarto scenes concatenated

This pairing is the whole point: a real copy-text and a real setting from it,
about 12,000 words, which is the only kind of evidence that can settle whether
a simulated compositor behaves like a historical one. See the Calibration
section of the manual.

Derived from transcriptions in the [Internet Shakespeare
Editions](https://internetshakespeare.uvic.ca/) (University of Victoria),
lightly normalised for line and speech-prefix handling. ISE texts are made
available for research and teaching; consult their terms before redistributing.
